What’s new

Version 1.1.0 key features

Released September 1, 2026
01

Voice messages

Natural voice-note communication for every user.

  • Record, preview, send and receive
  • Waveform and seek control
  • 1×, 1.5× and 2× playback
  • Microphone choice and noise suppression
  • Hands-free recording lock
02

Persistent conversations

Your local communication remains available after restart.

  • Per-profile message history
  • Preserved voice-note media
  • Atomic corruption-safe storage
  • Today, Yesterday and date separators
03

Reliable delivery

Clear status at every stage of a message.

  • Sending, Sent and Delivered states
  • Read receipts and group counters
  • Reconnect status reconciliation
  • Retry for failed messages
04

Offline queue

Temporary disconnection no longer interrupts the conversation.

  • Messages queue automatically
  • Send when the peer reconnects
  • Visible queued and failed states
  • Works with direct conversations
05

Resumable transfers

Large transfers continue instead of starting over.

  • Resume remaining file portions
  • Retain safe partial downloads
  • SHA-256 integrity verification
  • Progress and transfer controls
06

Message reactions

Respond quickly without adding another message.

  • Full-color emoji reactions
  • Real-time counts and updates
  • Reaction notifications
  • Text, voice and group support
07

Message actions

Useful controls exactly where you expect them.

  • Reply and Forward
  • Copy and Delete for me
  • Message details
  • Forwarded and reply previews
08

Search and filters

Find the right content without digging through history.

  • Messages, Voice and Files
  • Today and recent date ranges
  • Combined type/date filtering
  • Result-by-result navigation
09

Professional UI

A calmer, clearer interface designed for daily work.

  • Accessible Light and Dark themes
  • Readable typography and contrast
  • Compact content-sized bubbles
  • Consistent menus, fields and controls
